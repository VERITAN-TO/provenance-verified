# PROVENANCE VERIFIED™ — Screenshot Story (M3)

Six-screenshot sequence for App Store and Google Play.
Design: deep carbon background (#030506), protocol cyan (#20DDF2), machined silver (#B7C0C6).

---

## Screenshot 1 — Verify a Record

**Screen:** Scanner / Manual Entry

**Headline:** Verify in seconds.

**Sub-caption:** Scan the QR code on a certificate or physical item, or enter a record ID manually. PROVENANCE VERIFIED™ looks up the record and returns the trust result from the server.

**UI context:** Camera viewfinder centered on screen with a QR scan overlay. Below it, a "Enter ID manually" link in protocol cyan. App bar shows the PROVENANCE VERIFIED™ wordmark.

---

## Screenshot 2 — See the Trust Result

**Screen:** Trust Result

**Headline:** One clear trust tier.

**Sub-caption:** The server returns a determination — T1 through T4, or NOT QUALIFIED. See the tier, identity state, and freshness at a glance. Stale records are flagged automatically.

**UI context:** Trust badge displayed prominently (e.g., "T3 EVIDENCE-VERIFIED" in machined silver on a dark surface card). Below: Record ID, Subject ID, Identity state row. Tier badge border color reflects tier (graphite / bronze / silver / gold). Stale banner visible if freshness is not CURRENT.

---

## Screenshot 3 — Evidence and Limitations

**Screen:** Trust Result (scrolled) / Why This Tier

**Headline:** Know what supports the tier — and what doesn't.

**Sub-caption:** Review each claim verdict (SUPPORTED, ASSERTED, CONTRADICTED, REVOKED), the evidence items behind them, continuity state, and any limitations or prohibited inferences. Nothing is hidden.

**UI context:** Claims list showing labelled claim rows with colored state indicators. Evidence section below with source and integrity state per item. Limitations panel and PROHIBITED INFERENCES section visible. "Why this tier?" and "Why not higher?" action buttons at bottom.

---

## Screenshot 4 — Assess Reliance

**Screen:** Reliance Assessment (Actionability)

**Headline:** Before you act, assess your reliance.

**Sub-caption:** Select your intended purpose — Purchase, Resale, Insurance, or Public Marketing Claim. The server returns a live actionability decision: ALLOW, ALLOW WITH QUALIFICATIONS, or DENY. Qualifications and limitations are shown in full.

**UI context:** Purpose dropdown at top (selected: "Purchase"). Below: actionability result card showing "ALLOW" in green, or "ALLOW WITH QUALIFICATIONS" with qualification bullets. DENY and UNKNOWN render in red with a fail-closed warning. "Save Reliance Receipt" button active at bottom in protocol cyan.

---

## Screenshot 5 — Save a Reliance Receipt

**Screen:** Reliance Assessment — post-save confirmation

**Headline:** Record your decision.

**Sub-caption:** Save a reliance receipt that records the trust state digest, your purpose, and the actionability decision at the moment you assessed the record. Receipts are tied to the trust state — they are flagged if the underlying record changes.

**UI context:** Receipt saved confirmation card showing receipt ID, purpose label ("Purchase"), decision ("ALLOW"), and timestamp. "View Receipts" link in protocol cyan. The receipt is marked SERVER-ISSUED if the server confirmed it, or LOCAL-ONLY otherwise.

---

## Screenshot 6 — Receipt History

**Screen:** Receipt List / Receipt Detail

**Headline:** Your reliance history, on-device.

**Sub-caption:** Every receipt you saved is stored locally. Open any receipt to review the full context: the trust state digest at time of assessment, the purpose, the decision, limitations, and prohibited inferences. Stale receipts — where the underlying trust state has since changed — are clearly marked.

**UI context:** List of receipts showing record ID, purpose, decision badge, and date. One receipt expanded to detail view showing all fields. STALE receipt row visually differentiated with warning color.
