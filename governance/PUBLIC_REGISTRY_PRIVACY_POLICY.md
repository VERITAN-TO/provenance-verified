# Public Registry Privacy and Enumeration Policy

Pilot and Production registry search requires at least eight constrained public-ID
characters. Broad wildcard, lifecycle, organization, claimant, and evidence-source
enumeration is prohibited. Results are capped at 25 and use opaque time cursors.
Direct resolution by a complete public ID remains available, including stable
not-found responses that disclose no unrelated records. Sandbox may browse its
small deterministic fixture set and is visibly non-authoritative.
